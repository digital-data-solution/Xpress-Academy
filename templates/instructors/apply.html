Apply to teach

Applications are reviewed by a human — there's no instant approval. Once you're verified, we work with you to build the course together — you don't have to figure out course design alone.

What you'll bring, in whatever mix fits your subject

A course is built from lessons, and the platform supports several lesson formats — most courses mix a few of these:

  • Video — recorded lessons, demonstrations, screen recordings. Doesn't need to be studio-quality; clear and audible is enough.
  • Reading (text) — written lessons, explanations, step-by-step notes.
  • Images and diagrams — illustrations, charts, photos of real procedures/equipment where relevant — anything that makes a concept clearer visually.
  • Downloadable PDFs — checklists, worksheets, reference sheets, protocols learners can keep and reuse.
  • Live sessions — scheduled Q&A or demonstration sessions, if your course suits that.
  • Quizzes and a final exam — questions that check real understanding at the end of each module and the course, and unlock the certificate.

You don't need to have all of this ready before applying — this is just so you know what's possible once we start building. Tell us what you already have in the bio/credentials fields below, and we'll help with the rest.
